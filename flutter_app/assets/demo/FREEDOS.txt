FREEDOS DEMO IMAGE — PROVENANCE AND SOURCE
==========================================

FREEDOS.IMG was derived from the official FreeDOS 1.4 Floppy Edition,
published by the FreeDOS Project on 5 April 2025:

    https://www.freedos.org/download/
    https://download.freedos.org/1.4/FD14-FloppyEdition.zip

Official archive SHA-256:

    45b1fa7c52dd996c3bfa5e352ffcd410781b952a6ad629f15a4c9ec4bbaefc5a

The image contains only:

  * FreeDOS Kernel 2043 — KERNEL.SYS — GNU GPL v2 or later
  * FreeCOM 0.86 — COMMAND.COM — GNU GPL v2
  * FDCONFIG.SYS and AUTOEXEC.BAT, written for this app
  * RETRODEM.COM, the app's original MIT-licensed homebrew demo
  * the licence and provenance text files shipped beside this image

No Microsoft DOS, Microsoft Windows, commercial game, proprietary PC BIOS,
ROM dump, or third-party game data is present.

Complete corresponding source:

    FreeDOS kernel: https://github.com/FDOS/kernel
    FreeCOM shell:  https://github.com/FDOS/freecom
    Demo and image build recipe:
      https://github.com/CrownParkComputing/DosboxMultiplatform

The exact reproducible image recipe is tools/build-freedos-demo.sh. It
downloads the hash-verified official release, removes the installer and all
unneeded packages, and adds the original demo and licence files.
